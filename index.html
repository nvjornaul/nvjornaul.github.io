New Venezuela Jornaul was created with one mission: to carry a message straight into the trenches of the crypto world, reaching the degens who understand how a bloated government can end up hurting the very people it is supposed to protect.

A polarized Brazil

Today, Brazil is living through a period of extreme political polarization. We’ve reached a point where people celebrate when their political opponents are targeted, without even stopping to ask whether the same standards would be applied to their own side.

And this isn’t just about the left or the right.

The divide has become so deep that even those who claim to stand for freedom sometimes celebrate when that freedom is restricted for someone they disagree with.

Disagreement doesn’t make enemies

But political disagreement should never turn us into enemies. Regardless of our political beliefs, we believe that respect, freedom of speech, and due process must always come first.

Institutional concerns

We are also deeply concerned about Brazil’s institutional landscape. The concentration of authority in certain judicial matters, questions surrounding impartiality, and debates over the effectiveness of accountability mechanisms have left many Brazilians worried about legal uncertainty.

When people begin to lose trust in their institutions, society as a whole pays the price.

Not about elections

We are not here to challenge election results or make allegations of fraud without evidence. We are here to defend something that should stand above every political ideology:

Transparency. Justice. Freedom. And the right to question those in power.

A call for attention

We want the world to pay attention to what is happening in Brazil. And we are speaking out because we have genuine concerns about possible retaliation for expressing our views.

More than a memecoin

This memecoin is not a promise of wealth. It is a message, a community, and a way to bring attention to issues we believe deserve to be discussed.

We are not asking you to agree with everything we say. We are simply asking you to listen, question, and draw your own conclusions.
